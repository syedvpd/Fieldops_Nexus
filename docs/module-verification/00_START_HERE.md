## Super admin (platform level): how companies are created
The Super Admin is not part of any company. They create companies and invite each company's first owner.
1. The account is created once from the server command line (not from the browser):
   `python manage.py create_platform_admin --email <you@example.com> --full-name "<Your Name>"`, then set the password with `python manage.py changepassword <you@example.com>`.
2. Sign in as the Super Admin. Open **Platform → Organizations** (`/platform/organizations/`).
3. Click **New organization**. Fill: Name (e.g. `Gamma Works`), short code/slug (`gamma-works`), Owner full name, Owner email, Time zone (`Asia/Kolkata`), Country (`IN`). Save.
4. The system emails the owner an activation link. The owner opens it, sets a password, and is then the Organization Owner.
5. Check: the new company appears in the list as Active; the owner shows as "Invited" until they activate, then "Active".
(Suspending a company from the same screen blocks all its users; reactivating restores them. A company Owner never becomes a platform admin.)

## How the menu is organised (left sidebar, shown according to the user's role)
Administration (Organization, Users, Roles & Permissions) · Assets (Scan asset label) · Assets & Locations (Sites & Locations, Assets) · Coverage (Warranties & contracts, Expiring coverage, Providers) · Maintenance (Maintenance plans, Due & upcoming, PM history) · Operations (My Jobs, Incidents & Requests, Work Orders, Checklists, Inspections) · Inventory (Parts, Stock, Warehouses, Reservations, Movements) · Service levels (SLA tracking, SLA breaches, SLA profiles) · Service (Portal clients) · Insights (Dashboards, My work summary) · Compliance (Audit Trail, Compliance reports) · Overview (Dashboard).
A menu entry is hidden when the role has no permission; typing its address anyway shows **Access denied (403)** or **Not found (404)**. That is correct behaviour and part of the test.

## Rules that apply everywhere (test these in every module)
- Every button talks to the server: it checks who you are, which company, which record, and whether the step is allowed in the current status.
- Wrong order or missing information is refused with a red message in plain words (nothing half-saved).
- Every important action writes an audit entry (Compliance → Audit Trail).
- A user in company A can never see company B's records (404, same as a record that does not exist).
- Some users are limited to specific sites; they only see those sites' data.
