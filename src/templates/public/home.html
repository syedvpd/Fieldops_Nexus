How it works

From Request to Resolution

A clear, connected path from the first service request to the final sign-off. Every handoff accounted for.

  1. 01

    Request Raised

    A service request or incident is logged against an asset.

  2. 02

    Triage

    Severity, impact and SLA are assessed and approved.

  3. 03

    Work Order

    Approved work becomes a planned work order.

  4. 04

    Technician Assignment

    The right technician is assigned and dispatched.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Checklist, parts and labour are captured in the field.

  6. 06

    Supervisor Approval

    Completed work is reviewed before sign-off.

  7. 07

    Closure

    The record closes with a complete audit trail.
